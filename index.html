About Graphics Animations Interactive Web

I’m an experimental artist and designer with a professional background in technology, particularly digital identity and cloud infrastructure. My interdisciplinary practice explores how different mediums, including collage, photography, graphics, and animation, can be combined to reconstruct pedestrian experiences and reimagine the everyday. I’m also interested in revisiting folk and fairy tales from the Victorian era, exploring how these traditional narratives can be recomposed into contemporary digital experiences through animation, typography, and mixed media.

You can find me on Instagram, Substack, or Letterboxd.
